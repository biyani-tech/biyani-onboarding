# Before you join

About two hours of work, done before your first day. It means Day 1 is spent learning, not installing.

## Accounts

- [ ] **GitHub:** create a personal account at github.com if you don't have one. Turn on two-factor authentication. Send your username to your trainer so you can be added to our organisation.
- [ ] **Google account:** you'll use it to sign in to Google Antigravity, our main AI coding tool. Use the account your trainer tells you to.
- [ ] **WhatsApp:** share the number you want added to the team group.

## Your laptop/desktop

| | Minimum | Better |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Memory (RAM) | 8 GB | 16 GB |
| Free disk space | 30 GB | 60 GB |
| Operating system | Windows 10/11 (64-bit), macOS or Linux | Windows 11 or macOS |

Docker, an AI coding tool, VS Code and a browser together are heavy. If your laptop is at the minimum, tell your trainer before Day 1.

## Two short courses

- [ ] [Introduction to GitHub](https://skills.github.com) (GitHub Skills, under 1 hour)
- [ ] [Getting Started with Google Antigravity](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/getting-started-google-antigravity) (Google codelab, about 1 hour)

You can do them in any order. Note one question from each to ask on Day 1.
