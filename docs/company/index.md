# Who we are

Biyani Technologies is a software company from Kolhapur, founded in 2006. We build and support software for educational institutions (schools, colleges and universities) and for industries such as dairies, sugar factories and textiles.

## Vision and mission

These are in our own published words.

::: tip Vision
To empower institutes by providing a complete suite of software solutions and services that meet their evolving needs and growing business.
:::

::: tip Mission
To make available to the user the most sophisticated, yet user-friendly software. Our software aims to speed up and simplify not only day-to-day routine tasks but also the more complex procedures that need skill and judgement.
:::

## How we work

These are the working principles of our team. They sit alongside the vision and mission above, and we revise them as we learn.

- **Simple for the user, whatever it takes behind the screen.** A clerk in a college office should never need a manual. Complexity is our job, not theirs.
- **Built for the institutions we serve.** Patchy internet, tight budgets, exam-day rushes and Marathi-, Hindi- and English-speaking users are normal for our customers, so we design for them.
- **Student data is a trust, not an asset.** Much of our data belongs to children. We collect what's needed, protect it, and never feed it into tools that shouldn't see it.
- **Boring, proven technology.** We choose tools that will still be supported in ten years, so our customers never pay for our experiments.
- **Own your work, including what AI writes.** AI makes us faster. The person who commits the code is still responsible for it.

## Where we work

Our head office is in Kolhapur, with offices across India including Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, Jaipur, Bhopal, Raipur and Chandigarh, and we serve 700+ institutions.
