## Why we exist

We started in Kolhapur in 2006. Today colleges, universities, schools, milk unions and other industries across Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh run their daily work on our software.

> **Our vision:** to empower institutes by providing a complete suite of software solutions and services that meet their evolving needs and growing business.
>
> **Our mission:** to make the most sophisticated, yet user-friendly, software available to every user.
